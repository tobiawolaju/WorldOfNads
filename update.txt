## World of Nads — Development Update

### Completed

* Native Android client boots, logs in and plays on device.
* Cross-platform login: a WON account now persists on Android, so the same account works on web and phone.
* First Android build is out: a signed APK you can install straight on your phone, no store needed.
* In-match identity fixed: the name over your nad is your WON username, not a guest placeholder.
* Native dashboard brought in line with the web: real owned/equipped skins, match cards, 3D preview and level pill.
* Live match feed now shows who has the chicken and when they win ("X is holding the chicken", "X wins in 30s").
* Mobile controls: quicker joystick placement, auto-hide on release, and touch scrolling in the dashboard.
* Weapon feel pass: per-weapon muzzle FX, tracers, shake and phone haptics.
* Networking cleanup: the client talks to the live servers only (local fallback removed).

### Current Focus

**Native client polish and feel**

* Camera and collision behaviour on elevated parts of the map.
* HUD and joystick tuning on real devices.
* Performance on lower-end Android phones.
