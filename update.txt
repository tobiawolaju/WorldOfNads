## World of Nads — Development Update

### Completed

* Native Android client boots, logs in and plays on device.
* Cross-platform login: a WON account persists on Android, so the same account works on web and phone.
* First Android build is out: a signed APK you can install straight on your phone, no store needed.
* In-match identity fixed: the name over your nad is your WON username, not a guest placeholder.
* Native dashboard brought in line with the web: real owned/equipped skins, match cards, 3D preview and level pill.
* Live match feed now shows who has the chicken and when they win ("X is holding the chicken", "X wins in 30s").
* Mobile controls: quicker joystick placement, auto-hide on release, and touch scrolling in the dashboard.
* Weapon feel pass: per-weapon muzzle FX, tracers, shake and phone haptics.
* Networking cleanup: the client talks to the live servers only (local fallback removed).
* Reward flow hardened: XP credits in all rooms (including practice, settled off-chain), the dashboard Rewards tab and gameover show committed reward/MON state (no false prize promises), and settlement retries handle throttled responses.
* Experimental grab feedback: when the Action button would show GRAB, the nearest grabbable pickup is outlined in yellow (with its `line`/`box`/`MeshInstance3D` marker meshes tinted yellow) and the highlight clears on grab.
* **Friends/party (up to 4 players):** invite by username, accept/decline, ready-up, and a synchronized 5-second countdown that drops the whole party into the same match. Matches stay free-for-all; the party only groups players for launch.
* **Party UI on web and native:** invite slots live in the 3D preview overlay — `+` to invite, profile picture with the username underneath once invited, terminal-style `...` while pending, and a pink skewed leave button on the player. Invite/search popups are full-screen and follow the dashboard purple/pink theme.
* **Party state carries profile pictures:** members and pending invites now include the player's avatar so both clients render real pictures (initials fallback).
* **Glass chickens on the native dashboard:** the 3D preview is now ringed by iridescent bubble chickens matching the web ThreeScene look (fixed for Godot 4's normal matrix, tuned against the transparent preview viewport, 3x size).
* **Performance tuning:** low-processor mode, physics interpolation and Jolt tweaks shipped alongside the refreshed web export and Android APK.
