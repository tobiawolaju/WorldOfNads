# World of Nads - Angel Memo

*A gameplay-first competitive multiplayer arena, built for Web2 gamers*

**Founder:** Tobiloba Awolaju · **Ecosystem:** Monad · **Stage:** Live multiplayer demo, private beta · **Site:** worldofnads.xyz

## The game

World of Nads is a competitive multiplayer arena game built on one simple objective: fight other players for control of a chicken, and hold it for 60 seconds of possession time — or be holding it when the match timer expires — to win. Matches are short, movement-driven and decided by pursuit, evasion, positioning and timing rather than loadouts or spend.

## Why it works

The chicken is a deliberate risk/reward object. Carrying it is the only way to win, but it locks the carrier out of weapons and power-ups, turns them into a target who can be eliminated and robbed, and forces them to survive on movement, timing and improvised tools. A carrier can drop the chicken, pick up an object as a shield, or throw something to create space. Possession changes hands through confrontation as well as evasion, which is where the skill expression lives.

## Built for Web2 gamers first

The game is designed for regular gamers, not crypto natives. There is no wallet set-up, token purchase or blockchain knowledge required: players click and play in the browser or install the Android build. Blockchain (Monad) is optional supporting infrastructure, never a prerequisite for play, and cosmetic purchases do not affect competitive fairness.

## Where the product is today

Honest status, not a roadmap dressed as traction:

- Live multiplayer demo working on browser and Android against an authoritative Rust server.
- Implemented: real-time multiplayer sessions, account and profile flows, reward and XP crediting across rooms, including practice matches.
- In progress: end-to-end sponsored on-chain settlement and MON payout, a second playable map, retention analytics and research-driven UX.
- The beta is small and invitation-based; active-user numbers are reported only once verified.

## Business model (to be validated)

The primary monetization hypothesis is an optional $1 per month game payment, supported by cosmetic sales, sponsored tournaments and hosted community experiences. Four-month planning targets are 600+ monthly active players and at least 200 paying players at roughly $1 per month (about $200 MRR). These are targets, not forecasts.

## Risks

Retention and willingness to pay are unproven; end-to-end on-chain settlement is still in progress; contracts handling value will need review before launch; platform and regulatory requirements will be assessed before affected features ship. The beta exists to test these assumptions rather than assume them.
